# Privacy Policy

**Strabo Insight**

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what personal information STRABO INSIGHT LLC ("Strabo Insight," "we," "us") collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what control you have over it.

We are a small business. We collect very little, and we don't sell any of it.

---

## What we collect

**When you subscribe to the Strabo Signal**, we collect your email address through Beehiiv.

**When you use the contact form** on straboinsight.com, we collect your name, company, role, email, sourcing region, and message through Netlify.

**When you contact us** by email, we collect your email address and whatever you choose to tell us.

Payments for consulting engagements are invoiced separately. We do not store card numbers on this site.

**When you visit straboinsight.com**, our hosting provider processes standard server request data, including IP address, browser type, and pages requested. We do not run analytics on straboinsight.com. There is no Google Analytics, no tracking pixel, and no third party advertising or behavioural tracking of any kind. We cannot tell you which pages you visited.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

## Why we use it

- To deliver the Strabo Signal to you
- To respond when you contact us
- To send service messages about the Strabo Signal and changes to these policies
- To keep required financial and tax records
- To understand, in aggregate, which organizations and roles people who contact us come from, so we can improve the Strabo Signal

We send service messages because they're necessary to deliver the Strabo Signal. If we ever send marketing email, it will be separate and you can unsubscribe from it at any time.

## Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who need it to help us run the business, and only for that purpose:

| Provider | What they handle |
|---|---|
| Beehiiv | Collecting Strabo Signal email addresses and delivering the newsletter |
| Google Workspace | Hosting our email and delivering correspondence to you |
| Netlify | Hosting straboinsight.com and any form submissions |
| Wave | Bookkeeping records and consulting invoices |
| Zoho Sign | Electronic signature of consulting engagement agreements |

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or lawful government request, or where necessary to establish or defend a legal claim.

**We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not rent, trade, or otherwise disclose our subscriber list to anyone.**

## How long we keep it

We keep your Strabo Signal email address and contact details for as long as you are a subscriber, and for a reasonable period afterward in case you return.

We keep billing and transaction records for as long as required by tax and accounting law: generally seven years.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner, subject to those record-keeping obligations.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- know what personal information we hold about you
- get a copy of it
- correct it if it's wrong
- ask us to delete it
- opt out of any sale or sharing of it (we don't do either)
- not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights

To exercise any of these, email **chase@straboinsight.com**. We will respond within 45 days. We may need to verify your identity first, usually by confirming you control the email address.

If you are in Colorado, the Colorado Privacy Act may give you these rights, including a right to appeal a refused request. If we decline a request, we will tell you why and how to appeal.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

## Security

We use providers who maintain industry-standard security practices, and we limit access to personal information to what is necessary to run the business. We do not store card numbers on this site.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## International transfers

We operate from the United States, and our service providers are primarily US-based. If you subscribe from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection law may differ from that of your country.

## Changes

We may update this policy. If we make a material change, we will notify subscribers by email and update the date at the top of this page.
