# Strabo Insight Brand Kit

Icon direction: B, the Quadrant. Four-cell grid, one Gold cell, on a Navy tile.

## Folders
- icon/          Square icon. SVG masters + PNG at 16, 32, 48, 64, 180, 512, 1024.
- wordmark/      STRABO INSIGHT, EB Garamond Medium caps, 0.20em tracking. Text SVGs pull the font from Google Fonts; PNGs are pre-rendered.
- lockup/        Icon + wordmark, horizontal and stacked.
- social/        LinkedIn avatar (400), LinkedIn banner (1584x396), OG image (1200x630).
- tokens/        colors.css, colors.json, fonts.html.
- email-signature.html   Table-based signature for Gmail / Outlook. Host icon-navy-64.png and update the img src.

## Rules
- Icon always sits on its own Navy tile, including on Paper. Never recolor the Gold cell, never outline the tile, never rotate.
- Mono icons (single color, no tile) are for engraving, embossing, and one-color print only.
- Wordmark: Paper on Navy or Navy Deep, Navy on Paper or Paper Warm. Never Gold, never Slate.
- Minimum size: icon 24px (32px working size), wordmark 140px wide / 35mm.
- Clear space: the icon's width on all sides.
- No em dashes or en dashes in brand copy.
